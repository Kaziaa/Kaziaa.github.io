In vivo electrophysiology is the technique of **recording electrical signals from living cells** (usually neurons) inside a living animal, such as mouse, rat, or zebrafish.

Neurons communicate via electrical signals:
- **Action potentials (spikes)** — fast, around 1 ms
- **Local field potentials (LFP)** — slower population signals

Recording is done as voltage versus time and can be obtained from single neurons or groups of neurons.
