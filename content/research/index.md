My research focuses on understanding biological systems at the interface of  
**molecular biology**, **ion channel physiology**, and **computational analysis**.

A central theme of my work is uncovering how **ion channels and protein kinases**control cellular excitability and signaling, and how **structure-guided and data-driven approaches**
can be used to discover and optimize small-molecule modulators of biological targets. I am particularly interested in integrating **experimental biology** with **computational modeling, chemoinformatics, and data-driven approaches** to accelerate mechanistic insight and translational discovery.

<div style="clear:both;"></div>



### Kv7 (KCNQ) potassium channels
Kv7 channels are voltage-gated potassium channels that play a central role in
**controlling cellular excitability** in neuronal, cardiac, and metabolic
systems. My work focuses on understanding structure–function relationships,
functional modulation, and disease-relevant mechanisms that influence Kv7
channel activity.

### Protein kinases & cellular signaling
Protein kinases are key regulators of intracellular signaling networks.
I study how kinase-mediated signaling pathways interact with ion channel
function, contributing to both normal physiology and pathological dysregulation.

### Chemogenomics & data-driven discovery
Chemogenomics combines chemical biology with large-scale data analysis to
systematically explore target–ligand relationships. I use **structure-based
virtual screening, chemoinformatics, and machine learning** to identify and
optimize small-molecule modulators of biological targets.

## Key findings

- Identification of small-molecule modulators influencing ion channel activity  
- Experimental validation of computational screening hits  
- Structure–activity insights guiding lead prioritization
