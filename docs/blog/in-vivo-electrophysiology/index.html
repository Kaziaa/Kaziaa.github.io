Electrophysiology (in vivo)

In vivo electrophysiology

in vivo electrophysiology is the technique of recording electrical signals from living cells (usually neurons) inside a living animal (e.g. mouse, rat , zebrafish).

Listen to the electrical “Language” of the brain while it is live and functioning. Neurons communicate via electrical signals:

  • Action potentials (Spikes) – fast (~1 ms)
  • Local field potentials (LFP) – slow populations signals
  • Recording is done as voltage vs time and from single neurons or groups
