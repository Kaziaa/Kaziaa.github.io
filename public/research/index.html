Research

Ion channels, signaling, and data-driven discovery

Research overview

My research focuses on understanding biological systems at the interface of
molecular biology, ion channel physiology, and computational analysis.I am particularly interested in how molecular mechanisms translate into cellular and physiological outcomes, and how data-driven approaches can accelerate discovery.

Research themes

Ion channels & cellular excitability

  • Structure–function relationships of potassium channels
  • Electrophysiology and functional modulation
  • Disease relevance in cardiac and metabolic systems

Signal transduction & kinase biology

  • Protein kinases in cellular regulation
  • Crosstalk between signaling pathways
  • Mechanistic insights into dysregulation

Chemo-Genomics

  • Chemoinformatics and virtual screening
  • Structure-based drug discovery
  • Integration of genomics data with computational tools

Key findings

  • Identification of small-molecule modulators affecting ion channel activity
  • Experimental validation of computational screening hits
  • Structure–activity insights guiding lead optimization
